## Understanding Coco Maya's Private Event Pricing Structure

Private event costs depend on three main factors: group size, whether you're renting the whole space or part of it, and what day you're booking.

**Full Venue Buyout**

A complete buyout of Coco Maya's second-floor space accommodates up to 60 guests and requires a $3,500 food and beverage minimum. This covers the entire patio and interior bar area—you have exclusive use of the venue. The minimum applies whether you're hosting a Friday night birthday celebration or a Wednesday afternoon team lunch. Larger groups (61–80 guests with standing room) may negotiate higher minimums based on the specific event date and menu.

**Partial Patio Rental**

Not every event needs the whole venue. Partial patio rentals are perfect for groups of 20–40 guests and start at $1,500 food and beverage minimum. You get a dedicated section of the patio with reserved seating, and the rest of the venue remains open to regular guests. This option works well for smaller corporate events, engagement parties, or milestone birthdays where you want a semi-private feel without locking down the entire space.

**Day-of-Week Rates**

Friday and Saturday events command higher minimums than weekday bookings. Tuesday through Thursday private events often have lower minimums or more flexible terms, making them ideal if your guest list is smaller or your budget is tighter. Sunday afternoons and early evenings are moderately priced and popular for family celebrations.

## What's Included in Your Minimum Spend

The $1,500–$3,500 minimum covers food, wine, beer, and cocktails. You're not paying a separate rental fee on top of food and drink—the minimum *is* what you spend on the experience itself.

**Food & Beverage**

Work with our events team to design a menu tailored to your group. Options range from shareable appetizers and small plates to full seated dinners. Modern American coastal cuisine is our specialty, so expect fresh seafood, locally sourced ingredients, and thoughtfully crafted dishes. Cocktails and wine pairings are built into your spend, not added later.

**Bar Service & Staffing**

Our bartenders and servers are included. They'll manage the bar, take drink orders, and keep the event flowing. You don't hire or pay outside staff—it's all part of the package.

**Setup & Breakdown**

Tables, chairs, and basic décor arrangement are handled by our team. If you want to bring in a florist or add custom centerpieces, we can coordinate that. Linens and glassware are provided.

## Comparing Full Buyout vs. Partial Rental for Your Event Type

**Birthday Celebrations**

For a 30-person birthday dinner, a partial patio rental at $1,500 minimum makes sense. You get a reserved section, a dedicated server, and the flexibility to keep the vibe intimate without paying for empty space. A full buyout is overkill unless you're celebrating with 50+ guests or want complete privacy and control of the entire atmosphere.

**Corporate Events & Team Dinners**

Small team gatherings (15–25 people) fit comfortably in a partial patio rental. Medium-sized corporate events (40–60 guests) often justify a full buyout, especially if you're hosting a client dinner, year-end celebration, or product launch where brand presence and exclusivity matter. A full buyout also gives you more flexibility for presentations, networking, or a structured agenda.

**Bachelorette & Bachelor Parties**

These events typically benefit from a full buyout because the energy and celebration often extend across the entire space. A 40–50 person bachelorette party at $3,500 minimum works out to roughly $70–$87 per person for food and drinks—reasonable for a full evening in Little Italy with a curated experience.

**Rehearsal Dinners & Wedding-Related Events**

Partial rentals are popular for rehearsal dinners with 30–40 guests. If your wedding party is larger or you want the venue to feel exclusively yours, a full buyout is the way to go.

## How to Estimate Your Total Event Cost

Start with the food and beverage minimum, then factor in optional add-ons.
